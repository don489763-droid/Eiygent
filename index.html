Welcome to Sthembiso's website.


Sthembiso Khumalo was born at Natal Spruit Hospital. Natal Spruit was located at Gauteng. He then grew up at KwaNongoma at KwaZulu Natal where his mother was born at. Sthembiso never went to pre-school but started school at Holinyoka Combined Primary School. "I started from grade 1 and took 13 years to finish school but I have never failed." Sthembiso said.
